# Shift-aware biometric late reporting

The fix removes the timezone-dependent biometric overwrite so future biometric records remain governed by the Attendance pre-save hook’s employee-specific `shiftStart` and IST calculation. Both APIs derive raw late minutes for historical `Present/0` rows, with the attendance list applying the correction before filtering, sorting, pagination, and summaries. The correction is response-only and does not feed repaired history into salary/payroll calculations. Two edge cases keep report status and totals from fully matching the pre-save behavior.

Watch for: **confirmed (P2)** working-time totals can count lateness on Absent or Leave rows; **confirmed (P2)** punches less than 30 seconds after shift start remain Present in dynamically repaired history even though pre-save records the same punch as Late/0.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Biometric ingestion now stops after `att.save()`, leaving the model hook as the single writer of future status and raw lateness.

The attendance list repairs eligible `Present`, `Late`, and `Half Day` rows before filtering and pagination. Working-time applies the same calculation to every status, allowing incomplete-punch Absent rows and punch-bearing Leave rows to contribute to late aggregates.

Both report paths decide historical Late status from rounded minutes, while pre-save decides it from the unrounded timestamp. Punches one to twenty-nine seconds after shift start therefore differ between future and historical records.

The dynamic fields are not persisted. Payroll continues to consume stored attendance and apply its existing grace, paid-OT, and late-offset policies.

## Working-time repairs statuses outside the historical Present/0 target

`getWorkingTimeReport` computes `effectiveLateMinutes` whenever stored lateness is zero and a first punch exists, without checking `r.status` (`crm_backend/controllers/hrmsController.js:377-383`), then counts every positive result in `lateDays` and `totalLateMinutes` (`crm_backend/controllers/hrmsController.js:400-401`). This is **confirmed (P2)** because `finalizeDayAttendance` preserves an incomplete punch session while setting its row to `Absent` and `lateMinutes: 0`; a late incomplete punch therefore returns as Absent with positive late minutes and increments both aggregates. A Leave row retaining punch data has the same mismatch.

The attendance list only repairs `Present`, `Late`, and `Half Day` (`crm_backend/routes/attendanceRoutes.js:437-447`). The same row can report zero lateness in `/api/attendance` but positive lateness in `/api/hrms/working-time`. Both endpoints need the same eligible statuses.

## Rounded minutes lose the pre-save hook’s sub-minute Late state

Pre-save compares the exact IST punch minute, including seconds, with shift start and sets Late on any positive delta, then rounds `lateMinutes` (`crm_backend/models/Attendance.js:121-126`). Both report calculations round first and only promote historical Present when the rounded result is positive (`crm_backend/routes/attendanceRoutes.js:431-447`, `crm_backend/controllers/hrmsController.js:378-386`).

This is **confirmed (P2)** for punches 1–29 seconds after shift start: a 10:00:20 punch against a 10:00 shift is stored by pre-save as `Late/0`, but historical `Present/0` remains `Present/0` in both APIs. Late/Present filters consequently distinguish equivalent future and historical records. Keep an unrounded delta for status or adopt one explicit threshold in all three locations.
