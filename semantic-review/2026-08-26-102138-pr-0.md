## High-level view

Non-empty `sessions` is authoritative. Completed intervals are merged before the first-in/last-out span is reduced by the larger of configured lunch or positive gaps; open-only sessions return zero with `no-completed-session`, while completed intervals plus a trailing open session credit only completed work.

Payroll distinguishes zero credited time backed by a completed interval from zero backed only by an open session. The former enters half-day or shortfall classification, while the latter does not; no-session legacy punches and stored hours retain their compatibility paths.

Stored-hours-only source and quality now flow through attendance APIs, reports, UI/CSV, salary day notes and summary, shared preview/final salary data, and `SalarySlip` persistence.

Late and OT remain based on first-in and last-out clocks. Punch-backed records recalculate under the current lunch setting, configured zero remains valid, and stored-only values are not reduced again when settings change.

## Session evidence controls fallback and payroll classification

`calculateAttendanceTime` checks for any session facts before considering legacy aliases or cached `workingHours`. A non-empty set with no completed pair therefore remains `no-completed-session` despite stale fields, while valid intervals are sorted and merged before one `max(configured lunch, positive gaps)` deduction from the outer span.

`computeAttendanceAdjustments` now retains the calculator evidence and classifies either positive credited minutes or a completed interval. This sends a completed interval fully absorbed by lunch through half-day/shortfall handling without treating an open-only record as worked.

## Stored-hours provenance reaches every decision surface

Attendance serializers and working-time rows expose `workingTimeSource` and `workingTimeDataQuality`; attendance UI, CSV, master UI, and the working-time report consume the stored-only marker. Salary generation collects affected worked-status dates into `workingTimeDataQualityWarnings` on the shared `salaryData`; the breakdown adds day warnings and a summary, and final generation persists the same records in `SalarySlip`.
