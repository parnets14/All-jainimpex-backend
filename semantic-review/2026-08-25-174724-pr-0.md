# Shift-aware late reporting follow-up

The latest report changes resolve both prior P2 inconsistencies. Working Time excludes Absent and Leave rows from dynamic lateness and aggregates, while both APIs promote historical Present rows from the exact positive timestamp delta rather than rounded minutes. Representative 10:03, 10:06, 10:10, and 10:12 IST punches produce 3, 6, 10, and 12 raw minutes in both APIs. Biometric persistence remains IST-aware and the response-only repair does not alter payroll grace handling.

Watch for: no remaining P0/P1/P2 concerns in the scoped late-report changes.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

`GET /api/attendance` separates exact status detection from rounded minute display, so a positive sub-minute delta reports Late/0. `GET /api/hrms/working-time` now applies the same worked-status gate before reading punches or stored lateness, keeping Absent and Leave at zero and out of late totals.

Biometric ingestion leaves late derivation to the Attendance pre-save hook’s employee shift and explicit IST conversion. Payroll remains separate: it reads persisted punches, applies its existing employee/global grace, and does not consume either endpoint’s `rawLateMinutes` projection.

## Exact IST deltas across reporting and persistence

Both report paths base `isLate` on the unrounded positive difference and expose `Math.round` only as `lateMinutes`/`rawLateMinutes`; 10:00:01 IST therefore becomes Late/0, while 10:03/10:06/10:10/10:12 become 3/6/10/12. Working Time determines eligibility before selecting a punch or stored late value, so Absent and Leave cannot enter `totalLateMinutes` or `lateDays`.

`processBiometricPunches` performs no server-local status overwrite after `att.save()`. The model hook converts punch timestamps to IST before comparing the employee shift, and the report handlers do not persist their projected fields; payroll’s `lateBy > employeeGrace` calculation is untouched by these changes.
